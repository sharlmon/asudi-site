Security built in

Trust is part of the product

Many of our customers handle patient, member or student data. We treat that as a design constraint from the first day, not an add-on.

  • Encryption by default

    Data is protected in transit and at rest in everything we build.

  • Backups you can restore

    Regular backups with recovery steps written down and tested.

  • Every action logged

    AI agents record what they did, so you can always see and review it.

  • People stay in charge

    A human handoff on every agent, and humans make the clinical and legal calls.

  • Least access

    Each person and tool sees only the data its job needs.

  • Authorised testing only

    We run security tests only with written permission from the owner.
