Innovate. Integrate. Transform.

How every project moves

  1. 1

    Innovate

    Find the one job worth fixing.

    We look at how your team works and pick a single task where software will clearly help.

  2. 2

    Integrate

    Connect it to the tools you already use.

    WhatsApp, M-Pesa, email and spreadsheets stay in the picture, so nobody has to relearn their day.

  3. 3

    Transform

    Prove it, then grow what works.

    We measure the pilot with you. Only then do we build more, on the same shared platform.

How we work

Six principles we hold ourselves to

  1. 01

    Start narrow

    One job for one kind of business. Small enough to finish, specific enough to measure.

  2. 02

    Prove before you scale

    A small pilot with agreed measures comes before any larger build or commitment.

  3. 03

    Local by design

    M-Pesa, WhatsApp, SHA claims and Swahili are part of the brief, not an afterthought.

  4. 04

    Security from day one

    Sensitive data is a design constraint. Encryption, backups and logs come first.

  5. 05

    People in the loop

    Agents hand over to humans, and people make the clinical, legal and financial calls.

  6. 06

    Reusable, not one-off

    Services fund products, and products share one platform, so every project makes the next one faster.
