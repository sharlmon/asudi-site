How we work

Six principles we hold ourselves to

  • 01

    Start narrow

    One job for one kind of business. Small enough to finish, specific enough to measure.

  • 02

    Prove before you scale

    A 14-day pilot with agreed measures comes before any larger build or commitment.

  • 03

    Local by design

    M-Pesa, WhatsApp, SHA claims and Swahili are part of the brief, not an afterthought.

  • 04

    Security from day one

    Sensitive data is a design constraint. Encryption, backups and logs come first.

  • 05

    People in the loop

    Agents hand over to humans, and people make the clinical, legal and financial calls.

  • 06

    Reusable, not one-off

    Services fund products, and products share one platform, so every project makes the next one faster.
