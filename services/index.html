01

AI and Automation

Software agents that take over repeat jobs: answering messages, booking, chasing invoices and joining your tools together.

What we build

  • Single-job agents: invoice chasing, lead qualification, support triage
  • WhatsApp and voice receptionist for clinics, salons, garages and law firms
  • Back-office automation joining WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets and accounting tools
  • AI agent audit and governance

Where we start

One job for one industry, such as appointment booking for clinics, proven with a small pilot.

Who it is for
SMEs, clinics, distributors and professional firms.
What we watch for
A human handoff and a full action log on every agent. No medical or legal advice from the agent.
